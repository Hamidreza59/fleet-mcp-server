# Diagnostic Trouble Code (DTC) Reference

A condensed, synthetic field guide for fleet maintenance triage. All guidance is
illustrative and should not be used on real vehicles.

## P0301 — Cylinder 1 Misfire Detected

A misfire on cylinder 1 means combustion is incomplete or absent. Common causes
are worn spark plugs, failing ignition coils, clogged fuel injectors, or low
compression. Driving with an active misfire risks unburned fuel damaging the
catalytic converter and can leave a driver stranded. Recommended action: pull the
vehicle from service and schedule inspection within 24 hours. Start by swapping
the coil and plug for cylinder 1, then re-test under load.

## P0420 — Catalyst System Efficiency Below Threshold (Bank 1)

The catalytic converter on bank 1 is no longer converting exhaust gases
efficiently. This is frequently a downstream symptom of a chronic misfire or a
failing oxygen sensor rather than the converter itself. Severity is medium: the
vehicle is drivable but will fail emissions testing and fuel economy degrades.
Recommended action: resolve any active misfire codes first, verify the rear O2
sensor, then re-evaluate before replacing the converter (an expensive part).

## P0128 — Coolant Thermostat Below Regulating Temperature

The engine is not reaching its target operating temperature in the expected time,
almost always a thermostat stuck open. Impact is moderate: reduced fuel economy,
higher emissions, and a cabin heater that underperforms in cold weather.
Recommended action: schedule thermostat replacement within the week; not an
immediate safety issue but worsens fuel cost across a fleet.

## C0561 — System Disabled Information Stored (ABS/ESC)

The anti-lock braking / electronic stability control system has disabled itself
and stored a fault. This is a high-severity chassis code: the driver may have
reduced braking assistance. Recommended action: remove from service immediately
and inspect wheel-speed sensors, ABS module power/ground, and related wiring
before returning the vehicle to a route.
